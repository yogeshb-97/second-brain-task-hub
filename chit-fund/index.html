How it is worked out

Installment = chit value ÷ members. Prize = chit value − discount. The foreman's commission comes out of the discount; the rest is shared equally as dividend. Before and in your winning month you pay installment − dividend. After you win you pay the full installment.

Yearly rate is the true rate (IRR) of your payments against the prize. Winning early it is what the money costs you. Winning late it is what your savings earn.

Rules differ by chit group. Check your agreement for commission and dividend terms.
